# test4 Literature Search Log

## Search Question

What paired single-phase MMC-SMC studies report thermal resistance and pressure drop or pumping power at identifiable operating points?

## Search Terms

- `"manifold microchannel heat sink" "straight microchannel" thermal resistance pressure drop`
- `"manifold microchannel" conventional microchannel pumping power`
- `"additively manufactured microchannel heat sinks" manifold straight`
- `"hierarchical manifold microchannel" conventional microchannel thermal hydraulic`

## First-Pass Sources

- Collins et al. (2019), DOI `10.1109/TCPMT.2018.2866972`: paired AM SMC/MMC experimental candidate.
- Kong et al. (2021), DOI `10.1016/j.csite.2021.101583`: numerical MMC/conventional-microchannel comparison; use as context until matched points are checked.
- Lin et al. (2021), DOI `10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2021.121118`: MMC topology context, not a direct SMC control.

## Extraction Rule

No numerical point enters `reported_points.csv` from an abstract, a percentage improvement, or a secondary review. A point must identify the original source, figure/table, architecture, thermal-resistance definition, pressure boundary, and extraction method.
