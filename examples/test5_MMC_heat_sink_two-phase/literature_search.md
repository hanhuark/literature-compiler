# test5 Literature Search Log

## Search Question

What paired two-phase MMC-SMC studies report thermal resistance and pressure drop or pumping power at identifiable operating points, with phase condition and stability state retained?

## Search Terms

- `"manifold microchannel" "straight microchannel" flow boiling pressure drop`
- `"manifold microchannel heat sink" two-phase thermal resistance pumping power`
- `"hierarchical manifold microchannel" two-phase conventional microchannel`
- `"flow boiling" manifold microchannel straight channel thermal hydraulic comparison`

## First-Pass Sources

- Lin et al. (2021), DOI `10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2021.121118`: manifold-arrangement context spanning water single phase and HFE-7100 two phase.
- Luo et al. (2020), DOI `10.1016/j.ijheatmasstransfer.2020.119864`: two-phase manifold-arrangement context.
- Drummond (2018): open experimental dissertation on HFE-7100 MMC arrays with channel and overall pressure measurements.

## Extraction Rule

A boiling point cannot enter the thermal-resistance-versus-pumping-power plot without a source locator, pressure boundary, thermal reference, flow/phase state, and extraction method. Do not treat a reported maximum heat flux or a pressure-drop limit as a pumping-power point.
