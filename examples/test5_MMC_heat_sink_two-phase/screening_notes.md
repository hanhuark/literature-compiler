# test5 Screening Notes

## Why Two-Phase Data Are Separate

The thermal-hydraulic comparison is not governed solely by channel transition. At the same nominal flow rate, vapor generation and distribution can change void fraction, local mass flux, pressure drop, liquid supply, dryout, and instability. A two-phase literature point must retain these state variables.

## Required Fields Before Inclusion

- all test4 hydraulic and thermal fields;
- heat flux, mass flux, inlet pressure, and inlet subcooling when reported;
- an explicit phase/stability label, including pre-boiling, stable boiling, oscillatory/unstable boiling, partial dryout, or CHF/dryout;
- thermodynamic property source/state if volumetric flow is reconstructed.

## Interpretation Guardrails

- A lower pressure drop can coexist with poorer heat transfer because of changed wetted area, phase distribution, or a different dryout/stability boundary.
- Core and system pressure drops are not interchangeable.
- Treat the first-pass lack of plot-ready paired points as a search/extraction gap, not a negative finding about MMC performance.
