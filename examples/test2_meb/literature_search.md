## Inclusion Logic

Include papers that report at least one of the following for water boiling:

- MEB onset, transition, or stable MEB heat-transfer condition.
- Heat flux and wall superheat in an MEB or MEB-adjacent regime.
- Bubble/vapor oscillation frequency, pressure fluctuation, boiling sound, hydrophone, or AE signature.
- Reduced-pressure, confined, or microchannel MEB context that may explain BoilingLab observations.

## Exclusion / Context-Only Logic

Context-only papers include general acoustic boiling-crisis prediction, flow-boiling instability reviews, non-MEB enhanced nucleate boiling, or fuel-cladding AE monitoring unless they provide transferable signal-processing methods.
